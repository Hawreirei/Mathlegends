# Hero, item and rank data (issue #13)

Three JSON files in `src/data/`, each checked when it is loaded by [`src/systems/data.ts`](../src/systems/data.ts) (zod schemas). A bad file stops loading with a `DataError` that names the file and every bad field, for example:

```
Invalid heroes.json (2 problems):
  heroes.json: heroes[0].base.hp: Invalid input: expected number, received string
  heroes.json: heroes[0].role: Invalid option: expected one of "tank"|"fighter"|"mage"|"marksman"|"support"
```

`tests/data.test.ts` loads the committed files in CI, so a broken file fails the build. Unknown fields are rejected too, which catches typos such as `hpp`. All player-facing text needs both `id` (Indonesian) and `en` (English).

## heroes.json

Damage output x effective health (HP x (1 + armor/100)) stays within 1% between them at every level from 1 to 15 (`tests/heroes.test.ts`), so the lane is even and the bot's difficulty comes only from `bot.json` and its Year scaling (#43). Both names and designs are original (a test also checks the names against a list of well-known MOBA heroes; the full name check is #60).

### Adding a hero (JSON only)

1. Add an entry to `heroes.json` with every field below; `sprite` must be `hero-<id>`.
2. Run `npm run build:sprites`: the token is drawn from `look` (no code changes).
3. Point `playerHero` or `botHero` at it to play it.

`tests/heroes.test.ts` adds a third hero this way and plays a match with it.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `id` | Lowercase id, unique, e.g. `"nova"` |
| `name`, `title`, `lore` | `{ "id", "en" }`; `title` is a short epithet ("Star Strider") |
| `role` | `tank`, `fighter`, `mage`, `marksman` or `support` |
| `look.color` | `blue` or `red` (team colours in `theme.json`) |
| `look.emblem` | `sparkle`, `flower`, `moon` or `leaf` |
| `base.hp`, `base.mana` | Starting health and mana |
| `base.atk`, `base.armor` | Used by damage = ATK x 100 / (100 + ARMOR), see `docs/ECONOMY.md` |
| `base.moveSpeed` | Pixels per second |
| `base.attackRange` | Pixels |
| `base.attackSpeed` | Attacks per second |
| `base.hpRegen`, `base.manaRegen` | Per second |
| `growth.hp`, `.mana`, `.atk`, `.armor` | Added for each level above 1 |
| `skills` | Up to 4 skill ids from `skills.json` (3 skills, then the ultimate); see [SKILLS.md](SKILLS.md) |
| `sprite` | `hero-<id>`; generated by `npm run build:sprites` and listed in `docs/ASSETS.md` as `hero-<id>.svg` |

## minions.json (issue #20)

Stats per minion type, loaded and checked by `loadMinions` in `src/systems/data.ts`. When and how many spawn is in `economy.json` (`match`): first wave at 15 s, then every 30 s; 3 Infantry + 2 Lancers per wave; a Cannon every 3rd wave.

| Type | HP | ATK | Armor | Range | Attacks/s | Speed | Aggro | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Infantry (+) | 420 | 22 | 10 | 90 | 0.8 | 140 | 380 | Front line, melee |
| Lancer (×) | 300 | 28 | 4 | 300 | 0.7 | 140 | 400 | Ranged |
| Cannon (÷) | 820 | 45 | 25 | 340 | 0.5 | 120 | 420 | Tough, slow, ranged |

`spacing` (70) is the gap between minions in a fresh wave. `aggroRange` must be at least `attackRange` (checked).

Behaviour (`src/systems/minions.ts`): march towards the enemy base; target the nearest enemy **minion** in aggro range, or the nearest enemy **hero** only if no enemy minion is near; walk into range and attack; friendly minions push apart so a wave keeps its shape; dead minions do not respawn. All minions decide from the same snapshot each step and attacks land simultaneously, so list order gives neither team an edge: a test runs 10 minutes of mirrored waves and requires equal kills (99 vs 99) with the fight holding the middle. The player's killing blow on a minion counts as a **last hit** and fires `ml-last-hit` (used by the question overlay in #27).

## structures.json (issue #21)

| Field | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `tower.hp` / `armor` | 2400 / 40 | |
| `tower.atk` / `attackSpeed` | 140 / 0.8 | One shot every 1.25 s; about 118 damage to a starter hero (armor 18) |
| `tower.attackRange` | 420 | Pixels |
| `nexus.hp` / `armor` | 4000 / 40 | The Nexus never attacks |
| `dangerRingDistance` | 220 | Show an enemy tower's red range ring when the player is this close to its edge |
| `suddenDeath.enabled` / `afterSec` | true / 420 | Sudden death from 7:00 (#37) |
| `suddenDeath.extraDamagePerMinute` | 1.0 | Towers and Nexus take x2 damage after one minute of sudden death, x3 after two... |
| `suddenDeath.nexusDrainPerSec` / `drainPerTowerLost` | 0.003 / 1.0 | Each Nexus drains 0.3% of max HP per second x minutes of sudden death x (1 + towers that side lost) |

Rules (`src/systems/structures.ts`):
- **Protection order:** the outer tower can be attacked first; the inner tower only after the outer falls; the Nexus only after both towers fall. Protected structures show a soft white bubble.
- **Tower targeting:** nearest enemy minion in range, otherwise nearest enemy hero in range.
- **Minion targeting:** enemy minions, then attackable enemy structures, then enemy heroes, so waves push towers.
- **Win:** destroying the enemy Nexus ends the match (Victory); losing your own ends it (Defeat). The Result screen follows 1.5 s later so the collapse can be seen.

A simulation with blue waves only (no defenders, old tower positions) took the red outer tower at 103 s, the inner at 191 s and the Nexus at 238 s. With a hero on each side the lane stalls: before sudden death, no simulated match out of hundreds ended at a Nexus even in 20 minutes. With sudden death every match ends at a Nexus in about 9 to 11 minutes; see [BALANCE.md](BALANCE.md).

## bot.json (issues #24, #43)

The bot (`src/systems/bot.ts`). `base` holds every setting; each of the six `tiers` (one per Year) overrides some of them, and the match uses the step for the **player's Year** (`botForTier`). `newPlayer` is only for the simulator: a stand-in for a child in their first matches. `buildOrder` is the items the bot buys, in order.

| Setting | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `decisionIntervalSec` | Re-thinks this often (its reaction time) |
| `retreatHpFraction` / `returnHpFraction` | Walks home below this share of HP; comes back at the second |
| `engageRange` / `engageMinHpFraction` | Fights the player when this close and at least this healthy (aggression) |
| `acquireRangeBonus`, `holdBackPx`, `towerSafetyMarginPx` | Lane positioning (same at every Year) |
| `lastHitAccuracy` | Chance it takes a last hit it could take; otherwise it mistimes that one (#43) |
| `skillUseChance` | Chance it uses a ready skill when it has a reason to, at each decision (#43) |
| `defensiveSkillHpFraction` | Uses shield/buff-only skills below this share of HP, or when fighting (#43) |
| `itemDelaySec` | Waits this long after it can afford the next item before buying it (#43) |

| Year | Reaction | Last hits | Skill use | Item delay | Engage range | Fights from HP | Retreats below HP |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.85 s | 30% | 5% | 240 s | 220 | 85% | 50% |
| 2 | 0.65 s | 50% | 20% | 140 s | 270 | 72% | 42% |
| 3 | 0.48 s | 68% | 40% | 80 s | 320 | 62% | 36% |
| 4 | 0.36 s | 80% | 55% | 45 s | 370 | 55% | 30% |
| 5 | 0.27 s | 90% | 72% | 20 s | 420 | 48% | 27% |
| 6 | 0.18 s | 100% | 95% | 3 s | 480 | 38% | 22% |

Each decision, in order: a minion it can kill in one blow (unless it mistimes it), then the player (if the engage conditions hold), then the weakest enemy minion in reach, then an enemy tower or Nexus when its own minions are taking the tower's shots (#43), else hold the lane behind its minions. It casts skills with the same rules as the player (damage skills when the target is in range, the ultimate first against the hero; shield/speed skills when hurt or fighting) and buys `buildOrder` items. Its dice are seeded, so a match replays the same way in tests.

### Difficulty check (simulator)

`tests/botDifficulty.test.ts` plays the new player (answering 60% of questions right) against each Year's bot, six 10-minute matches each, and compares structure damage done minus taken ("push", a destroyed tower = 1) and the gold lead:

| Bot Year | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| New player's push | +0.49 | +0.35 | +0.05 | -0.50 | -0.26 | -0.51 |
| New player's gold lead | +343 | +250 | +42 | -84 | -306 | -359 |

The tests require the Year 1 bot to be beatable (push > +0.2, gold lead > +150), the Year 6 bot to be a real challenge (push < -0.2, gold lead < -150), and the gold lead to fall every Year.

**Finding for #37 / #50:** in these bot-against-bot matches the lane is slow: about one tower falls in 10 minutes and no Nexus falls even in 20, against the 8 to 10 minute target (GDD section 3). Children who push on purpose will be faster than the simulated player, but tower toughness (`structures.json`) and the sudden-death decision should be checked in the balance simulator (#37) and the playtest (#50).

## minions.json: heroBodyRadius

`heroBodyRadius` (46) keeps minions at least their radius plus this far from any hero, so a hero is never hidden under a wave. Infantry can still hit heroes (26 + 46 = 72 < 90 range).

## The shop (issue #35)

Rules in [`src/systems/inventory.ts`](../src/systems/inventory.ts) (pure, tested in `tests/inventory.test.ts`); screen in [`src/ui/ShopPanel.ts`](../src/ui/ShopPanel.ts).

- **Open any time** (GDD decision 1): the **Shop / Toko** button at the right end of the top bar, or **B** on a keyboard; **Close / Tutup** or **Esc** closes it. The match keeps running, and a question card still appears above the shop.
- **Tabs:** All, Attack, Magic, Defense, Move, Support. Item cards show a category icon (blade, star, shield, wing, leaf: shapes, not just colours), the name and the cost (gold when you can afford it).
- **Buy:** pick an item, then **Buy · 300**. It costs the item's price and fills the first free slot. The button says **Not enough gold (300)** or **Slots full** instead when it cannot.
- **Sell:** tap one of your items in the slot row, then **Sell · 210**: 70% of the price back (`economy.json` `shop.sellRefund`, rounded down), and the slot empties.
- **6 slots** (`economy.json` `shop.slots`); the same item can be bought more than once.
- The details pane shows the item's stats and, in gold, its passive.

## Item effects (issue #36)

[`src/systems/items.ts`](../src/systems/items.ts) (pure, tested in `tests/items.test.ts`):

- **Stats stack.** A hero's stats are base + growth x (level - 1) + every item's stats + active skill buffs. Two Training Swords give +24 ATK. Buying applies the stats at once and selling takes them away; extra max HP or mana also fills the current amount by the same number, and selling clamps it. Items stay through level-ups.
- **Unique passives count once** however many copies you own (two Comet Spears: +52 ATK but still 18 on-hit). A non-unique passive would add up. Cooldown reduction from all items is capped at 40%.
- **Where each effect applies:** on-hit damage and lifesteal on basic attacks (lifesteal heals a share of all damage the attack dealt); cooldown reduction and skill power when a skill is cast; the low-HP shield is checked every step.
- Items are not part of the skill damage budget, which is measured "with no items" (docs/ECONOMY.md).
- The bot buys items from #43; until then only the player has them.
- Spending and refunds change only the gold you hold, not the earned-gold totals used by the math-share cap (#28).
